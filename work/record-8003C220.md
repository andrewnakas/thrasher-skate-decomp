func_8003C220 walks a 0x1C-stride table downward from `D_8008B2F0 * D_8008B2F4 - 1`,
adding the delta to every live u16 count. Two levers closed it, both new to the record:

1. **Index the table, do not offset the pointer.** `e = base + i*0x1C; e->count` compiles
   to `addu` + `addiu v1,v1,2` + `lhu 0(v1)`; the game wants `addu v1,v0,v1; lhu 0x2(v1)`.
   Keeping a table base (`tbl = (Ent *) D_8008B2FC`) and writing `tbl[i].count` keeps the
   field offset as a displacement on the base. 20 -> 12 differing words in one step.
2. **Truncate inside the add to stop reassociation.** `a0 + e->count - 1` is reassociated to
   `(a0 - 1) + count` and the constant is hoisted out of the loop (`addu a0,a0,0xFFFF` before
   the loop). Writing `a0 + (u16) (e->count - 1)` makes gcc materialise the constant with
   `ori 0xFFFF` in the `bltz` delay slot and emit the game's two `addu`s in order. 12 -> MATCH.
